TEAM STORY

ZEPHYR RACING

PROJECT MANAGEMENT • NATIONAL FINALS

Structured planning, disciplined execution, and a strong finals run.

Zephyr Racing's story is one of organisation and ambition. With TRS mentorship, the team sharpened its project management, design reviews, and race preparation process so each technical decision was grounded in measurable performance and realistic timelines.

  • National Finals qualification: the team combined technical execution with clear communication to build a credible and consistent competition entry.
  • Design review culture: iterative testing and structured feedback helped improve safety, reliability, and overall vehicle performance.
  • Competition readiness: from pit display to technical presentations, the team built a complete racing package that stood out on stage.

WILD RACING

FASTEST CAR • WORLD FINALS QUALIFIER

A performance-first team that turned testing into results.

Wild Racing benefited from focused technical mentoring in testing methodology, vehicle refinement, and on-track reliability. Their approach balanced speed with robustness, using a highly disciplined optimisation process to improve lap performance without losing consistency.

  • Fastest car award: the team delivered one of the strongest performance results of the season and proved its pace under competition conditions.
  • 3rd overall finish: a clear sign of outstanding reliability, preparation, and race-day execution across all judging categories.
  • World Finals qualification: the result confirmed the team's technical maturity and validated TRS's support model for high-performance student racing.

TEAM STORY

NOVA TRACK

WOMEN IN MOTORSPORT • TEAMWORK

Excellence built through collaboration, inclusion, and technical ambition.

Nova Track demonstrates how the right team culture can multiply technical potential. TRS helped the group strengthen communication, leadership, and cross-functional cooperation so that design, manufacturing, and presentation objectives aligned behind one shared goal.

  • Women in Motorsport Award: the team reflected TRS's commitment to opening doors for new generations of women in engineering and motorsport.
  • Teamwork-led performance: clear roles and stronger collaboration improved consistency across design, testing, and presentation work.
  • Multidisciplinary growth: the team created a model where technical skills and communication developed together rather than separately.

FUTURE GOALS

BUILDING THE NEXT GENERATION OF
STEM RACING EXCELLENCE.

Our first season proved that student teams can compete at a high level when they are supported by strong mentorship, disciplined project management, and a clear ambition to grow. The next chapter is about scaling that model across Île-de-France and preparing more young engineers to compete internationally.

→
GOAL 01

EXPAND THE MENTOR NETWORK

Recruit more engineers, academics, and industry mentors to support students across design, manufacturing, and competition strategy.

→
GOAL 02

GROW OUR TEAM PORTFOLIO

Increase the number of student teams we guide so more young people can access STEM racing, technical leadership, and hands-on engineering experience.

→
GOAL 03

REACH HIGHER ON THE GLOBAL STAGE

Build toward stronger performances in national finals, deeper technical maturity, and a sustainable path toward international competition.
